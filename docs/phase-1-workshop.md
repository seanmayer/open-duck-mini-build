# Phase 1 Workshop Setup

Phase 1 is about building a practical starter workshop before buying the expensive Open Duck Mini robot components. The goal is to have enough tools and consumables to print parts cleanly, install inserts, assemble hardware, solder basic wiring, and debug simple electrical issues.

## Status

Phase 1 is considered complete enough to move on to robot-specific parts.

## Optional Items

These are useful, but they should not block Phase 2.

| Item | Priority | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Digital multimeter | High if not already owned | Useful for continuity checks, battery voltage, power rails, and finding shorts before powering the robot. |
| Silicone soldering mat | Medium | Protects the desk and keeps tiny screws and connectors organized. |
| Blue threadlocker, such as Loctite 243 | Medium | Useful once the robot is walking and vibration becomes a real issue. |
| Velcro cable ties | Medium | Nice later for reusable cable management; nylon cable ties are already available. |
| Magnetic parts tray | Low | Nice quality-of-life item for M2 and M3 screws. |
| Additional silicone wire gauges | Later | 26-28 AWG for tiny signals; 18-20 AWG for higher-current battery wiring. |
| Perfboard or prototype PCB kit | Later | Useful for custom circuits, but not required for the stock Open Duck Mini build. |
| JST or Dupont crimp kits | Later | Add when connector work becomes a real need. |

## Phase 1 Completion Criteria

- Core mechanical tools are available.
- Heat-set inserts and fasteners are ready.
- Soldering setup is ready.
- Wire stripping, solder rework, flux, heat shrink, and silicone wire are available.
- A basic photo record of the Phase 1 tools has been added to `photos/phase-1-tools/`.
- Robot-specific purchases are deferred until the official BOM has been checked.

## Safety Notes

- Use the soldering station in a ventilated area.
- Keep soldering tips clean and tinned.
- Use electronics-safe rosin/no-clean flux only.
- Check polarity and continuity before connecting batteries or power supplies.
- Treat lithium batteries carefully and buy reputable cells, holders, BMS modules, and chargers during the power phase.
